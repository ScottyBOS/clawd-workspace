### Enoshima & Kamakura Highlights

**Must-See:**
- ✅ **Great Buddha (Kōtoku-in)** — Iconic 13.35m bronze statue, outdoor seating inside
- ✅ **Hase-dera Temple** — Hillside temple with caves, gardens, ocean views
- ✅ **Enoshima Shrine** — 3-shrine complex on island, sacred to Benzaiten
- ✅ **Enoden Railway** — Scenic coastal train (Ghibli vibes!)
- ✅ **Komachi Street** — Kamakura's main food/shopping street

**Optional (if time/energy):**
- Hokoku-dera (Bamboo Grove Temple) — Add 30 min from Kamakura Station
- Enoshima Caves (Iwaya) — ¥500, sea caves on island west side
- Shichirigahama Beach — Classic Fuji + beach photo spot

**Food Recommendations:**
- Kamakura: Menman (tsukemen), Komachi Soba, street food (croquettes, matcha soft serve)
- Enoshima: Seafood bowls, sake lees soft serve (local specialty)

**Packing for Day 4:**
- Comfortable walking shoes (lots of temple steps!)
- Light jacket (coastal areas = windier)
- ¥1,000 cash for temple entries + offerings
- Enoshima-Kamakura Freepass (keep accessible)
- Camera (scenic Enoden rides!)
